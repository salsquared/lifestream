vendored: P0.6

Committed on purpose, not gitignored. Two reasons:
  1. the app must load the world offline — no CDN fetch at runtime;
  2. the P1 country seed reads country names from this file's feature properties,
     so the seed is only reproducible against a fixed geometry vintage.

50m, not 110m: 110m carries ~177 features and the authored groupings reference 237
country codes, so 110m cannot represent the data.

Re-vendoring: bump world-atlas in client/package.json, re-copy the file, update the
version above, and re-run the country seed — feature ids/names can move between vintages.
