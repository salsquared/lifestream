Committed on purpose, not gitignored. Two reasons:
  1. the app must load the world offline — no CDN fetch at runtime;
  2. the P1 country seed reads country names from this file's feature properties,
     so the seed is only reproducible against a fixed geometry vintage.

50m, not 110m: 110m carries ~177 features and the authored groupings reference 237
country codes, so 110m cannot represent the data.

Re-vendoring: bump world-atlas in client/package.json, re-copy the file, update BOTH the
version and the sha256 above, and re-run the country seed — feature ids/names can move
between vintages. The sha256 is what makes that procedure checkable rather than merely
described; verify a copy with:

  shasum -a 256 client/public/topojson/countries-50m.json

A mismatch means the file drifted from the recorded vintage, and the seeded country ids
can no longer be assumed to line up with what the client renders.
