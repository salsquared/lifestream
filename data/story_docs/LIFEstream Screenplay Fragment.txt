EXT.- BEGINNING OF TIME


White burst of light leads to a red starburst in the center. The white background fades and reveals a white grid that expands into itself faster and faster. The red starburst begins to fade with a noisy transition into a sphere that grows into the background as the grid continues to grow.


FEMALE VOICE
The closest thing to the beginning of the universe we can deduce is what we call the Big Bang.


A figure of the age of the universe appears on the lower left. It's slowly speeding up from a planck time as the frame evolves.


FEMALE VOICE
It's the equivalent of turning your hands back on a clock with your fingers.

EXT. INSERT SHOT


A low angle establishing shot of a white minimalist clock with black accents.


Cut to a zoomed in view from an even narrower angle. An index finger comes into frame at normal speed and moves the hour hand of the clock counter-clockwise from 10 to 12. Loud clicks are heard in reverberating as the finger forces the gears inside the clock to advance backwards.


Female Voice
Like how you'd know the hand of a mechanical clock went over the 2 if it’s back past the 1.

EXT. - BEGINNING OF TIME 


The “universe” continues to slowly unfold as previously described as photos of schematics of different parts and depth of the clock's mechanics flash on screen.


Female Voice
All that's required to see this as a possibility is to observe that we know for certain that energy/matter exists - that is to say we exist - and that it had to start somewhere. This is known as the Anthropic Principle.


The energy density of the universe was so high moments after creation, that no singular particles as we know them today existed. This immaterial substance can be described as the purest and simplest form of energy in a uniform universe. 


CUT TO:


A glowing holographic panel shows readings for a fuel tank. They are 100% full.




Female Voice
With this unfathomable energy density came the equally unthinkably fast expansion of the space inhabited by this energy. This era is known as Inflation.

As the universe continues to cool, approximately 3 to 20 minutes after the beginning, we reach the epoch of Nucleosynthesis or the creation of the first atoms.
