Act 1:
1. SQ: Rustbucket Test Flight
   1. SC: Rustbucket in Death Valley desert before dawn.
      1. X sits in his scrappy spaceplane in a dried Mojave lakebed pre-dawn, glowing eyes in a dark cockpit
      2. X commands AL to begin an audio recording
   2. SC: Flashback of X’s School —X’s Old Middle School
      1. Flashback: a child puts on neural gear at a classroom desk
   3. SC: Big Bang Visualization — School HoloVision Lesson
      1. Teacher's voice intros a cosmology lesson — "how you and I got here" — with a religion-disclaimer aside
      2. A 3D grid flickers in, rotates, collapses inward; red glow blooms at center
      3. Sphere ignites red-white, goes transparent, blue and yellow streaks shoot outward — Big Bang
   4. SC: Test Flight from Mojave Floor to LEA
      1. Intercut: X tells AL to start the reactor ignition SQ
      2. AL counts down, X braces, "Ignition!" — craft launches
      3. AL relays flight info through the Gs; reactor cuts off; X flips the craft upside-down relative to Earth
   5. SC: Low Earth Atmosphere/Inside Rustbucket Cockpit
      1. X takes in the view of Neo LA sprawling across SoCal, Earth Tower 1 spearing out of Disaster Ridge — a beat of peace
      2. Adan radios in, scolds X for the delivery delay, invokes their father's memory to twist the knife
      3. X ends the call, annoyed; reclines and tells AL to head to the usual drop-off (abandoned gas station, edge of Neo LA)
      4. Before AL can confirm, the craft punches through a glowing blue radiation cloud
      5. Catastrophic electrical failure; AI knocked out; craft tumbles into ballistic reentry
      6. X yanks on an oxygen mask above his head.
   6. SC: Memory/Flashback
      1. Breathing hard, eyes closed — flash: memory of his mother's asphyxiation, his own phobia, the DIY mask ripping off
      2. Flash: his father in military uniform, North Korean War — snaps X back
   7. SC 4B: Inside Rustbucket Cockpit
      1. X unbuckles, reaches behind his seat, hits the ignition restart
      2. A few thousand feet up the craft comes back to life; AL is back online


2. SQ: Downtown Neo LA Chase
   1. SC: X falls out of the sky
         1. AL: LAPD Anti-Terrorist Division intercepts in 2 minutes
   2. SC: Main Chase Sequence
      1. X turns toward the city instead of away; weaves past buildings and automated craft lanes
      2. LAPD cuts into the cockpit speakers on emergency freq, orders surrender
      3. X uses the reactor upgrade to outrun every cruiser — Akira-style chase montage
         1. S:
            1. FR: Cut: interior of the last cruiser; the officer calls in death bots
      4. X notices the cruisers fall off, slows to look for a landing spot
      5. Cockpit screen cracks from an ion round; AL reorients away from the line of fire
      6. AL: three LAPD death bots, AI-piloted, ion cannons that bust tanks; one more hit and they're gone; first shot was a probe to ID human vs AI pilot
      7. Cabin alert: acid rain storm closing from the opposite direction
      8. X turns 180° upwards, straight toward the storm
      9. AL panics; X asks how fast they have to go for the bow shock to flash the acid to vapor before it reaches the hull
      10. "Mach 4!" — and warns that the g-load to get there and hold the line will knock X out
      11. X hands control to AL — no human could fly the burn conscious — reclines and braces as the reactor spools up
      12. The craft accelerates under AL's hands, leaving the death bots firing wildly behind; stacked sonic booms hammer past as it crosses Mach 4; X greys toward blackout under the Gs
      13. At the storm wall the shock layer vaporizes the incoming acid; AL threads the craft straight through
      14. The Rustbucket punches out the far side — nose and leading edges pitted and scarred, but intact — and vanishes into the mist
      15. The death bots follow at their slower intercept speed; too slow to raise a shock sheath of their own, they're caught bare in the acid and dissolve
   3. SC: X lands and hides the Rustbucket
      1. X coasts to the city outskirts, lands, hides the Rustbucket
      2. X transfers the vials Opal from the storage compartment to a satchel — still intact, still within spec


3. SQ: GZVA Donation — Old Korea Town
   1. SC 1: Random Bus Stop — Near Socal Hills
      1. X walks to a bus stop to wait for his ride back into the city
   2. SC 2: GZVA — Old Korea Town
      1. A well covered man is dropped off with a duffel bag in front of an old rundown building in downtown LA in the rain. 
      2. A wet and well covered figure knocks on a door inside the building. 
      3. At first the door opens slowly with a distrustful eye peeking through and quickly letting out a sigh and greeting to the figure in the doorway. 
      4. The figure steps in before taking his hood off and revealing it's X. 
      5. X delivers Opal to his dad's best friend and the ground zero veteran's association from his military days. 


4. SQ: X returns home to Min-Seo and Adan — Castaneda House & Garage
   1. SC: X explains what happened to Adan and Min-Seo
      1. X returns home to shock from the room.         
      2. His girlfriend is anxiously waiting at the door from the chase and scolds him. 
      3. Adan asks what happened. 
      4. X explains and takes some drops when he ends the conversation. 
   2. SC: Opal Trance
      1. The first trance-like SC takes place. 
      2. Trance transforms to memory. 
      3. X is confronted with the memory of his mother's death briefly before waking up. 


5. SQ: Castaneda Garage
   1. SC: X prepares for the stream — Castaneda Garage
      1. The dream transitions to X getting ready to broadcast an illegal LIFEstream
      2. Adan helps X connect the broadcast umbilical cord to the back of X’s head.
      3. Adan wishes X good luck and rushes back to his desk to monitor the situation. 
   2. SC: Audience Members Introduction
      1. A quick montage of the audience of people that consume illegal live LIFEstreams.
      2. A teenager excitedly sets up his older brother’s system
      3. A security guard at work has a non-LIFEstream, livestream of the event on his HoloVision while at work
   3. SC: X streams his virtual race and lived experience — Castaneda Garage & Tunnels
      1. X starts up the Rustbucket. 
      2. The custom DFD engine starts up, bringing the room to life. Wind and light bounces everywhere while it starts up. 
      3. X grabs the LIFEstream broadcast connector and inserts it into the back of his head. 
      4. Adan establishes communication and checks LIFEstream connection and server healthchecks
         1. A quick montage of the hardware involved like the Neuputer, LS Connector, LS Broadcast Server, etc. 
      5. Checks clear and Adan starts to countdown to LS broadcast and race start. 
      6. Adan ends the countdown and X takes off into the tunnels. 
      7. We see on Adan’s HoloVision the different feeds coming in.
         1. First we see X's actual view with metrics of the stream and X’s bio data around it.
         2. Then we see a rendering of an environment where the camera moves like X does in real life.
         3. The third and final view is a composite of the two. It's X’s experience superimposed into the virtual world. 
         4. As C catches speed Adan informs him that they're live and X floors it.
