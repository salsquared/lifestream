In the latter part of the 21st century, the motorsport racing industry faced an unprecedented decline in viewership, overshadowed by emerging, high-risk sports drawing adrenaline-driven audiences. In a collective bid to reignite the global passion for racing, leading motorsport companies initiated the creation of a novel racing spectacle. Taking ion-fusion technology from their space subsidiaries and enabling crafts to attain unprecedented speeds and altitudes, culminating in the ion-Fusion League (iFL). 
Despite immense efforts to attract elite drivers to race for the iFL, the endeavor seemed doomed following a catastrophic incident. Max Lauda, renowned as one of the most exceptional drivers in motorsport history, met a tragic end in an ion-fusion craft accident. His demise cast a long shadow on the iFL, discouraging professionals, who questioned the viability of mastering crafts even Lauda couldn't control.
In a desperate quest to salvage the league and under coercive pressure from his superiors, Motoaki “Moto” Nagai, the leader of Seki Sports Ion Racing Division, ventures beyond traditional racing circles to recruit the enigmatic X, compelling him to drive for Seki in the inaugural season of the iFL. In six months, they must construct a formidable team, develop their state-of-the-art racecraft, and conquer tracks from the earth's surface to its low earth orbit to win life-changing levels of fame and fortune.
